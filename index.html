SIH 2026 Presentation Flow

2:50 Min Live Judge Presentation Storyline

A step-by-step walkthrough covering citizen booking, collector digital weighing, and ULB compliance.

Scene 1: Ramesh (Citizen) — Doorstep Request & AI Scan 0:00 - 0:45

Ramesh uploads an image of plastic bottles and cardboard. The FastAPI AI model auto-detects categories, looks up guaranteed minimum rates, and broadcasts a pickup to nearby collectors.

Scene 2: Suresh (Collector) — Voice Navigation & Digital Scale 0:45 - 1:40

Suresh taps "बोलकर सुनें" to hear spoken Hindi instructions. He accepts the job, inputs certified weights with large +/- buttons, and receives immediate wallet credit (₹449).

Scene 3: Municipal ULB (NDMC) — EPR Compliance & CPCB Export 1:40 - 2:30

Admin inspects aggregated daily tonnages, approves pending informal collectors for official ID cards, and exports an audit-ready CPCB EPR certificate.
